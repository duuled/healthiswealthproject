## Two recipes that stretch every dollar

**Red Beans and Rice**

This is one of the most complete, satisfying meals you can make for under $8. The dried red kidney beans do the heavy lifting — they're cheap, filling, and when you cook them slow with the right aromatics, they develop a depth that tastes like it took all day. (It takes about an hour of active attention. The rest is hands-off.)

The keys: don't skip the smoked sausage if you can get it, bloom your trinity (onion, celery, bell pepper) with a bay leaf and thyme, and let the beans get just creamy enough that some of them start breaking down and thickening the pot naturally.

**The Jerk Chicken Marinade Trick**

The trick isn't in the marinade itself — it's in the time. Most people marinate for 30 minutes. The flavor you're chasing requires overnight. The scotch bonnet, the allspice, the thyme — they don't just sit on the surface. They break down the proteins and get inside the meat. 24 hours changes everything.

If scotch bonnets aren't available in your area, habaneros are the closest substitution. Don't use jalapeños. The flavor profile is completely different.

A whole chicken, a bag of rice, some beans, and a few pantry spices: that's $12 at most grocery stores. That's four people eating real food.
