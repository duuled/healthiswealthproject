## Why we built this

My grandma never wrote anything down. Not a single measurement, not a single note card tucked in a drawer. I watched her cook my whole childhood — standing on a step stool just to reach the counter, watching her hands move like she was born doing it — and I still couldn't replicate her mac and cheese. Not even close. Every time I tried, something was off. The texture, the depth, the way the edges crisped up just right. I couldn't figure out what I was missing.

That's why we built Flavor Crave. Because the food we grew up eating deserves to be preserved. The recipes that fed us at Sunday dinners, holidays, block parties — the ones our grandmothers, aunts, and tías carried in their heads and their hands — those recipes belong on paper. We're just finally writing them down.
