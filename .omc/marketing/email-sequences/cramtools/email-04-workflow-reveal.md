---

## Step 1: The brain dump (45 minutes)

Monday morning, first thing. No editing, no filtering. I open a blank document and write everything I want to say this week.

- Topics I've been thinking about
- Questions I got from clients or followers last week
- Something I read that I have a take on
- A process or lesson from a recent project

This is ugly. Incomplete sentences. Half-formed ideas. That's fine. The goal is to get everything out of my head and into a document. By the end of 45 minutes, I have 500-800 words of raw material.

**Tool used:** Plain text editor. Literally nothing fancy here.

---

## Step 2: AI expansion (90 minutes)

This is where Claude comes in. I take the brain dump and run it through a structured prompt that asks Claude to:

1. Identify the 5 strongest distinct ideas in the document
2. Expand each one into a standalone social post (LinkedIn format)
3. Pull the most substantial idea and expand it into a full 800-word article draft
4. Generate 3 short-form video scripts (60-90 seconds each) from the remaining ideas

The output isn't finished content. It's a strong first draft for everything. I read through it, cut what doesn't sound like me, tighten the language, and add specifics that only I would know. That editing pass takes about 30-40 minutes total.

**Tool used:** Claude (claude.ai) — specifically Claude 3.5 Sonnet for this workflow. The context window handles the full brain dump without issues.

---

## Step 3: Organization and scheduling (45 minutes)

All the finished pieces go into Notion AI. I have a content calendar database with fields for platform, format, publish date, and status. Notion AI helps me write captions and adjust tone for each platform — LinkedIn gets the professional version, other platforms get something shorter and more direct.

From Notion, I push everything to Buffer or Later for scheduled publishing. 15 minutes of clicking, and the whole week is queued.

**Tool used:** Notion AI for organization and caption adjustment, Buffer for scheduling.
