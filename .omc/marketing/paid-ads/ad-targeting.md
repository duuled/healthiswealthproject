## Retargeting Audiences

### RT-1 — Site Visitors (All Pages, Last 30 Days)
- **Exclusion:** Purchasers
- **Message angle:** "Still thinking it over?" + WELLNESS10 code
- **Ad sets to run:** Ad Set 1 (WELLNESS10) and Ad Set 5 (Power 5 bundle)
- **Frequency cap:** 3 impressions per week per user

### RT-2 — Add to Cart / Initiate Checkout (No Purchase, Last 14 Days)
- **Message angle:** Abandoned cart recovery — urgency + discount
- **Copy example:** "You left something behind. Use WELLNESS10 before it expires."
- **Frequency cap:** 5 impressions per week per user

### RT-3 — Video Viewers (75%+ completion, TikTok or Meta)
- **Message angle:** Move warm audience to product page
- **Ad sets to run:** Whichever video they watched — product-matched follow-up
- **Creative:** Static image ad with direct product CTA (lower friction than another video)

### RT-4 — Email List Custom Audience (Klaviyo sync)
- **Segment:** Non-purchasers who opened email in last 30 days
- **Message:** Personalized "Your first order" push with WELLNESS10
- **Lookalike base:** Use this list to build LAL-2 (see below)

| Lookalike | Source | Similarity | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| LAL-1 | Purchaser list (all time) | 1% | Highest value prospecting |

| LAL-3 | Video viewers 75%+ | 2–3% | Broad awareness, low CPM |
| LAL-4 | Add-to-cart (no purchase) | 1% | Intent-signal prospecting |
| LAL-5 | /start page visitors | 1–2% | Funnel-aware prospecting |

**Recommendation:** Run LAL-1 and LAL-2 head-to-head in the first 30 days to identify which source produces lower CPA.

## Placement Recommendations

| Placement | Priority | Best Ad Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Feed (4:5) | High | Single image, carousel |
| Instagram Reels | High | 9:16 video, 15–30 sec |
| Facebook Feed | Medium | Single image, carousel |
| Instagram Stories | Medium | 9:16 static or video |
| Facebook Reels | Low (test) | Repurposed TikTok content |
| Audience Network | Off | Too low quality for conversions |

**Recommendation:** Start with Instagram Feed + Reels only. Expand to Facebook Feed after first 7 days of data. Use Advantage+ placements off until you have baseline ROAS data.

## Budget Allocation Guide

### Starter Budget ($50/day total)
- Prospecting (cold): $35/day (70%)
- Retargeting: $15/day (30%)

### Growth Budget ($150/day total)
- Prospecting — LAL audiences: $60/day
- Prospecting — Interest-based: $50/day
- Retargeting: $40/day

### Scale Trigger
Increase budget by 20% when:
- ROAS > 2.5 for 3 consecutive days
- CPA is below $28 (target CPA for initial order)
- Frequency stays below 2.5 for prospecting campaigns

## A/B Testing Priority Queue

Run these tests in order as budget allows:

1. **Hook test (TikTok):** Question hook vs. statement hook vs. controversy hook — same body copy
2. **Headline test (Facebook):** Benefit headline vs. social proof headline vs. curiosity headline
3. **CTA test:** "Shop Now" vs. "Get the Bundle" vs. "Start Today"
4. **Audience test:** Interest-based Segment A vs. LAL-1 (purchaser lookalike) — same creative
5. **Format test:** Single image vs. carousel — same copy, Ad Set 2 (Sea Moss)
6. **Discount display:** Code WELLNESS10 shown in creative vs. code revealed only at checkout

## Compliance & Ad Policy Notes

- All ads must link to healthiswealth.live/disclaimer
- Do not use before/after images that imply weight loss in ad creative (Meta policy)
- Avoid "cure," "treat," "diagnose," or "prevent" in all ad copy
- Health claims must use qualifying language: "supports," "may help," "has been shown to"
- Testosterone-related claims (Shilajit) must be general wellness framing, not clinical
- Sea Moss ads referencing thyroid should say "supports thyroid function" not "treats thyroid conditions"
- Use "Sponsored" label — never disguise as organic content in static ads
