## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Pillars
1. **Investigative** — We dig beneath the surface. We ask the questions mainstream outlets skip.
2. **Artistic** — The podcast uses elaborate, intentional sound production. Journalism as craft.
3. **Community-Rooted** — We serve Southern California residents directly affected by the stories we cover.
4. **Accessible** — Complex policy and tech stories made clear for regular people, not insiders.
5. **Independent** — Not beholden to advertisers or political interests. Honest reporting.

### Tone by Context
- **Newsletter / Blog posts:** Journalistic but warm. Clear headlines. First paragraph answers: who, what, why it matters to you.
- **Podcast scripts:** Narrative-driven, cinematic pacing. Scene-setting before the facts.
- **Social media:** Hook with the local angle first. "This affects you if you live in LA."
- **Headlines:** Specific, news-driven. No clickbait. No vague teases.

### Voice DON'Ts
- Never sensationalize or exaggerate
- Never use jargon without explanation
- Never bury the local impact — always connect national/state stories to LA community
- Never write passive, corporate-sounding journalism
- Never ignore underrepresented community perspectives in LA coverage

## Newsletter Strategy

### Publication Format
- **Platform:** WordPress (liveliveradio.wordpress.com) + cross-post to email subscribers
- **Frequency:** Weekly (target: consistent day/time for habit formation)
- **Format:** 3–5 stories per issue. Lead story gets full treatment. 2–4 briefs below.
- **Audio companion:** Each newsletter issue pairs with a podcast episode

### Newsletter Structure Template
```
SUBJECT LINE: [City/Topic] + [Compelling angle] + [Local impact hook]
Example: "Speed cameras are coming to LA — here's what the city isn't telling you"

OPENING: 2–3 sentences. What happened. Why it matters to this reader specifically.

MAIN STORY: 300–500 words. Context → Facts → Community impact → What to watch next.

BRIEFS (2–4 items):
- [Headline]: [1–2 sentence summary + source link]

PODCAST NOTE: "This week on The Shore News podcast: [topic]. Listen here → [link]"

FOOTER: Standard unsubscribe + social links
```

### Subject Line Formulas
- `"[City/neighborhood] just [action] — here's what it means for you"`
- `"The story nobody's covering about [topic] in LA"`
- `"Why [local issue] matters more than you think"`
- `"[Number] things happening in coastal LA this week"`
