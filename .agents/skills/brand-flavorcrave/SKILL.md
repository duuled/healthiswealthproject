## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Pillars
1. **Cultural Pride** — We celebrate the food traditions of Black, Latino, Caribbean, and West African families. Food is identity.
2. **Real Talk** — We're not food snobs. No $40 ingredients. No pretentious plating. Real food for real families.
3. **Technique Over Trend** — We teach the WHY behind recipes, not just the steps. Layering, timing, seasoning — the techniques that actually matter.
4. **Nostalgia + Modern** — Connect the emotional memory of grandma's kitchen to the practical reality of a 30-minute weeknight.
5. **Community First** — We're building a kitchen table, not a cooking show.

### Tone by Context
- **TikTok / Reels:** Storytelling-first, emotional hook, "real person" energy, never overly polished
- **Pinterest:** SEO-optimized titles, warm and inviting, save-worthy, descriptive
- **Blog:** Conversational authority — teach and inspire simultaneously
- **Email:** Feels like a text from a friend who's a great cook

### Voice DON'Ts
- Never write in a "food blogger" cliché voice ("This dish literally changed my life!")
- Never ignore cultural context — always acknowledge the origin of a dish
- Never use overly formal or clinical language
- Never make it seem difficult — always position recipes as achievable
- Never lean into diet culture or "healthy version of" framing unless explicitly the angle

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP
- **Age:** 25–45
- **Identity:** Black, Latino, Caribbean, West African women and men who grew up with home cooking as culture
- **Location:** Urban US markets + diaspora communities
- **Psychographic:** "I want to cook the way my family cooked, but I'm busy and I never learned the recipes properly."
- **Pain points:** No written recipes from family, lack of technique, limited time, kids who won't eat bland food

### Secondary ICP
- Multicultural millennials discovering their food heritage
- Home cooks who follow food TikTok and want to level up
- Parents who want to feed their families real, flavorful food without restaurants

### Buyer Awareness
- **Unaware → Problem aware:** "Why does my food taste flat?" → technique education content
- **Problem aware → Solution aware:** "I can't recreate my grandma's recipes" → cultural recipe content
- **Solution aware:** Regular home cooks looking for new dishes → recipe catalog + weekly meal plans

## Content Pillars

1. **Technique Education** (30%) — The WHY behind cooking. Layering, seasoning, heat management.
   - Format: TikTok explainers, "the secret ingredient" reveals, before/after comparisons
2. **Cultural Recipe Documentation** (30%) — Soul food, Caribbean, West African, Mexican, Southern recipes
   - Format: Step-by-step blog posts, TikTok demos, Pinterest recipe cards
3. **Budget Cooking** (20%) — Real meals under $10 for a family. Dignity without sacrifice.
   - Format: "$X feeds a family of 4" TikToks, budget roundup blog posts
4. **Viral Recipe Hacks** (20%) — Remakes, dupes, "better than [restaurant]" versions
   - Format: TikTok comparison videos, Pinterest "copycat" SEO content

## Viral Content Frameworks

### "The Comparison Reveal"
Hook: "$35 restaurant [dish] vs $8 homemade. I can't believe nobody talks about this."
Body: Show the expensive option → reveal better/cheaper homemade version
CTA: "Recipe link in bio"

### "The Secret Technique"
Hook: "Nobody in cooking shows tells you this one technique."
Body: Reveal a professional-level technique (layering, blooming spices, deglazing)
CTA: "Follow for weekly technique drops"

### "The Budget Challenge"
Hook: "$10. Family of 4. Watch."
Body: Show entire meal prep from $10 grocery haul
CTA: "Save this. Try it this week."

## Design & Visual Direction
- **Aesthetic:** Warm, rich food photography. Deep colors, steam, close-up texture shots.
- **Color palette:** Warm oranges, deep browns, rich greens. Never cold/clinical.
- **Font feel:** Approachable, not fancy. Sans-serif for clarity.
- **Video style:** Real kitchen, natural lighting, no over-produced studio feel.
- **Avoid:** Flat lay food styling, white marble backgrounds, "influencer aesthetic"
