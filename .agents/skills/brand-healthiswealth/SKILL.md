## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Pillars
1. **Rooted** — We connect modern wellness to ancestral traditions. Reference history, culture, and lineage.
2. **Direct** — No fluff. No vague wellness-speak. We say exactly what something does and why.
3. **Empowering** — We believe in self-advocacy in health. We educate, not prescribe.
4. **Community** — We speak to and for our community (Black, Latino, multicultural wellness consumers).
5. **Anti-hype** — We call out overpriced, overhyped products. We're the honest alternative.

### Tone by Context
- **Social media / TikTok:** Conversational, bold hooks, cultural references, real talk
- **Product pages / shop:** Confident, benefit-led, science-backed, trust-building
- **Blog / educational:** Authoritative but accessible, cite studies, no jargon
- **Email:** Personal, warm, community-first, always deliver value before the ask
- **Ads:** Urgent but honest, specific claims, social proof heavy

### Voice DON'Ts
- Never say "miracle" or "cure" — we make no medical claims
- Never use generic wellness buzzwords without substance: "holistic," "transform," "journey"
- Never be preachy or lecture the audience
- Never imitate Erewhon-style luxury wellness (we're the affordable alternative)
- Never use passive voice in headlines or CTAs

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- **Age:** 25–45
- **Identity:** Black, Latino, and multicultural adults who grew up with food as medicine
- **Location:** Urban US markets (LA, Atlanta, Chicago, Miami, Houston, Brooklyn)
- **Income:** $40K–$90K household
- **Psychographic:** "I want real results, not pharma. I trust my grandma's remedies AND modern science."
- **Pain points:** Chronic fatigue, brain fog, poor immunity, high supplement costs, distrust of mainstream brands

### Secondary ICP
- Fitness-focused men 28–42 (testosterone, performance, recovery)
- Health-conscious women 25–40 (skin, immunity, hormonal balance, gut health)
- Parents optimizing family nutrition

### Buyer Awareness Levels
- **Unaware:** Doesn't know superfoods exist as supplements → lead with transformation/outcome
- **Problem-aware:** Tired, sick often, low energy → lead with problem agitation
- **Solution-aware:** Knows about sea moss / moringa → lead with quality differentiation
- **Product-aware:** Comparing us to competitors → lead with proof and value
